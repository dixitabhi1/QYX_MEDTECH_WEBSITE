MISSION & FOUNDING PURPOSE

Closing the male-factor gap in reproductive healthcare.

For decades, reproductive medicine has placed the diagnostic and emotional burden almost exclusively on women. Yet epidemiological data shows male factors are involved in nearly half of all infertility cases.

Qyx Medtech was founded to eliminate that imbalance: starting with a specialized consultancy platform connecting men directly with vetted specialists in male reproductive health.

Clinical Architecture Phase 1: Consultancy Network

Eliminating stigma and diagnostic fragmentation through direct 1-on-1 access to verified reproductive andrologists.

THE CLINICAL WEDGE

Why a platform model comes first

Before launching complex diagnostics or at-home testing hardware, a credible network of expert doctors must exist.

1

The Stigma Barrier

Men face pervasive cultural hesitation, shame, and clinic anxiety when seeking fertility advice. Providing confidential, 1-on-1 virtual consults with verified urologists lowers the friction to take the first clinical step.

2

Doctor-First Foundation

A diagnostics tool without specialized medical support leaves patients with confusing numbers and no clear path forward. Building the doctor consultancy network first ensures clinical guidance is immediately available.

3

Clinical Autonomy

We operate as an administrative and technological partner for physicians. Doctors maintain absolute diagnostic and clinical independence, free from arbitrary platform quotas.

DATA GOVERNANCE

Privacy, compliance, and clinical ethics

Male fertility data is deeply personal. Our technological infrastructure is engineered around rigorous privacy frameworks from day one.

✓

Working Toward ISO 27001 Certification

We are actively structuring our information security management system (ISMS) around ISO/IEC 27001 international standards to safeguard clinical data integrity.

✓

HIPAA-Aligned Data Protocols

Consultation metadata, appointment notes, and encrypted video streams are architected in adherence with health information privacy best practices.

✓

DPDP Act Compliant Architecture

Operating in strict compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, providing explicit user consent controls, clear purpose limitation, and the right to data erasure.

✓

Academic & Clinical Research Discussions

We are in ongoing exploratory discussions with leading institutions including IIT Madras and AIIMS Patna for collaborative research initiatives in male reproductive health algorithms. Formal partnership milestones will be announced following mutual execution of institutional agreements.

CLINICAL LEADERSHIP

Founded on medical board integrity

Rather than displaying simulated advisor headshots or placeholder bios, Qyx maintains a strict policy of authentic credential verification. Our clinical advisory committee comprises certified urologists, reproductive endocrinologists, and medical researchers currently vetting our Phase 1 physician onboarding pipeline.
