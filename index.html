THE WIDER CLINICAL PICTURE

Male fertility can be influenced by many factors.

A specialist considers medical history, hormones, infections, medicines, lifestyle and environmental exposure alongside semen analysis results.

01 •

Health History

Previous illness, surgery, infections, long-term systemic conditions and family medical history may all be relevant during an evaluation.

02 •

Hormones & Medicines

Hormonal changes (testosterone, LH, FSH) and certain prescription medicines or treatments can directly alter spermatogenesis or function.

03 •

Daily Habits

Sleep quality, chronic stress, tobacco, alcohol, heat exposure (saunas, tight clothing), and physical fitness provide useful diagnostic context.

04 •

Environment

Workplace heat, chemical pollutants, heavy metals, microplastics, and radiation exposures may be clinically relevant depending on circumstances.

Note: These factors do not determine fertility on their own. A qualified specialist clinician assesses them in context alongside medical history and clinical test results.
