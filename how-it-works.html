END-TO-END STORYBOARD

The 6-step doctor-to-patient journey

How the entire ecosystem connects — from credential intake to ongoing clinical care.

1

Doctor applies

A specialist fills out the intake application with credentials, board registration, and clinical focus.

2

Verified by Qyx

Our clinical committee verifies state medical council registration, active license, and clinical background.

3

Profile goes live

The doctor's consultancy profile becomes visible and bookable with their chosen hours and direct fee structure.

4

Patient finds a match

A visitor searches by language, specialty, or schedule, or is routed confidentially from the triage intake form.

5

Consult is booked

Patient books directly; both sides receive calendar confirmations, encrypted video links, and automated notifications.

6

Consult happens

Encrypted 1-on-1 video consult takes place; lab report analysis and structured clinical follow-up continue on Qyx.
