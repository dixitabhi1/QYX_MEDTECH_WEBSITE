CREDENTIALING STANDARDS

Verification requirements

To preserve clinical trust for every patient, every specialist on Qyx must fulfill four core standards:

1

Medical Degree & License

Recognized medical degree (MBBS/MD/MS/DO) and valid national medical council registration.

2

Clinical Specialization

Documented specialization or fellowship in Urology, Andrology, or Reproductive Endocrinology.

3

Independent Practice

Minimum 2 years of independent clinical practice in male reproductive healthcare.

4

Clinical Orientation

A 15-minute intro call with our medical advisory team to review platform standards and ethics.
